Your picks vs PickNet

Sealed: open six packs of the chosen set and build a 40-card deck. PickNet rates every card in the pool (how much a drafter would want it as a first pick), and the deck model, fit on 17Lands Sealed games, searches the pool for the deck it expects to win the most games with.

PickNet is a transformer trained on 17Lands drafts to predict which card a human takes. It runs entirely in your browser and drives the 7 bots at the table. Packs are real opening packs from 17Lands drafts.
